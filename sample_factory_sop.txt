# VELVET GARMENTS FACTORY: STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP)

## SOP Section 2.1: Safety Equipment & Access
All personnel entering the floor must wear rubber safety boots and safety goggles. 
- Extra safety helmets and safety goggles are kept in Store Room B near Gate 2.
- If Store Room B is locked, backup safety gear and helmets can be retrieved from Locker 3 in the Supervisor's office.

## SOP Section 4.2: Machine 4 Reset Procedure
In the event that Machine 4 shuts down automatically and flashes a Red Error E-02 warning light, follow these reset steps:
1. Turn off the main breaker switch at the end of Line 2.
2. Wait exactly 30 seconds for pressure valves to cool down.
3. Flip the main breaker switch back to ON.
4. Press the green Reset button on the control panel.
Caution: Do not attempt to reset more than twice. If the error persists, contact the shift supervisor immediately.

## SOP Section 8.4: Keys & Supervisor Allocations
- Keys for Store Rooms A, B, and C are kept by Supervisor Ramesh Kumar on-site.
- During off-shift hours, backup access keys can be obtained from the Duty Coordinator at Safety Gate 2.

## SOP Section 12.3: Chemical Spill & Leak Protocol (HIGH RISK)
In the event of a chemical leak or gas spill (specifically near Aisle 4 or Aisle 5):
1. Immediately press the emergency gas cutoff valve located next to the main exit.
2. Alert Supervisor Ramesh Kumar immediately at safety extension line 201.
3. Evacuate the area immediately. Do not touch or attempt to clean the spilled chemical without protective hazmat gear.
